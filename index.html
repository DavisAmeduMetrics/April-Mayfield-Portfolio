Executive Bio

April Amedu is an innovative Brand Strategist and creative operator who transforms consumer-product concepts into nationally recognized brands. With a foundation in high-volume operations and experiential storytelling, she blends creative direction with data-driven e-commerce strategy to drive measurable brand growth. As the Founder and Brand Creative Lead of Glaze-ish, a playful, dessert-inspired skincare brand, April directed end-to-end creative development and secured national retail placements within her first year. Her diverse operational background—ranging from managing teams of over 100 associates as an Amazon Operations Area Manager to delivering immersive hospitality experiences for The Walt Disney Company—equips her with a unique ability to bridge creative vision with scalable execution. Currently, she continues to develop premium brand identities, recently launching the jewelry brand Gummy Gem Studio and utilizing AI-assisted mockups to conceptualize retail experiences like Wash the Wag.

About Me

My approach to brand strategy sits at the intersection of immersive storytelling and meticulous operational execution. Early in my career, I honed the art of audience engagement and themed storytelling while performing in immersive entertainment environments across Walt Disney World and the Disney Cruise Line. I later transitioned to high-volume logistics as an Operations Area Manager for Amazon, where I managed large teams and identified critical process-improvement opportunities to present to senior leadership.

I merged these two disciplines—creative experience and operational scale—to found Glaze-ish. I built the brand from the ground up, defining everything from its visual identity and sensory-led messaging to managing multichannel e-commerce storefronts across Shopify, Amazon Seller Central, and TikTok Shop. Whether I am optimizing digital marketing analytics to refine Meta ad campaigns, designing AI-assisted creative assets for my premium jewelry brand Gummy Gem Studio, or conceptualizing a retail identity, my goal is always to craft brand experiences that captivate consumers and drive tangible growth.
